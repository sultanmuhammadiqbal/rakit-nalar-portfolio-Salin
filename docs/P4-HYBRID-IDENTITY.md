# P4 — Hybrid Personal × Studio Positioning

## Brand architecture

**Rakit Nalar Studio** = the studio-facing identity for presenting thoughtful website development, reusable digital systems, and AI-assisted workflow experiments. **Creator** = a real professional identity to be approved separately. The creator may be a solo operator, but do not assert a team, incorporated business, registered trademark, prior paid-client roster, or specific personal credentials without evidence.

### Preferred interim positioning

> Rakit Nalar Studio adalah studio digital independen yang mengeksplorasi website, sistem produk reusable, dan workflow berbantuan AI. Portofolio ini mendokumentasikan proyek eksperimen yang dibuat, diuji, dan diperbaiki secara terbuka. Identitas profesional kreator dan kontak bisnis akan diumumkan setelah diverifikasi.

### Content framework

- **Homepage hero:** explain *what gets built* and show one verified independent case study. Do not promise measured business results.
- **Selected work:** showcase Ruang Reka as fictional independent engineering demonstration. Source PRs/CI are evidence of engineering work, not client outcomes.
- **About:** use studio-first voice and factual ownership status. `creator: null` is a valid pending state; no fake profile/photo.
- **Contact:** while no verified email exists, use `Baca studi kasus` and `Lihat GitHub`, not `Diskusikan ide` or fake `mailto:`.
- **Services:** describe capabilities being developed; do not assert availability or fixed prices until capacity, deliverables, and contracts are decided.

### Launch-time owner decisions (do not auto-fill)

| Decision | Safe current value | What user must supply or approve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Creator name | Not displayed / pending | Full professional name or explicit anonymous policy |
| Creator role | Independent digital studio | Approved title and employment/conflict-of-interest review if necessary |
| Photo or portrait | None | Owned photo, permission to publish |
| Contact email | Disabled | Verified mailbox and publication consent |
| Team size | Not asserted | Genuine arrangement; never imply agency workforce |
| Social/portfolio links | Evidence-linked GitHub only | Owned handles and visibility review |
| Client testimonials | None | Consent and verifiable provenance |
| Commercial offer | Exploratory | Terms, actual scope, pricing and capacity |

## Copywriting guardrails

Permitted: `proyek eksplorasi independen`, `studi kasus fiktif`, `hasil pengujian CI`, `konfigurasi reusable`, `belum diverifikasi`.

Avoid: `dipercaya banyak klien`, `agency berpengalaman`, `meningkatkan penjualan X%`, `tersertifikasi`, `terbukti 100 di semua perangkat`, or a named founder absent approval.
