This document is a reviewable commercial baseline for Rakit Nalar Studio. It does not publish an offer, activate ordering, authorize outreach, create a contract, or approve deployment. The existing website must continue to show no public price and no active contact channel until the owner gives separate, explicit approval.

## Website UMKM Bundle

The proposed price excludes domain, hosting, paid third-party services, and work outside the written scope. Final pricing remains subject to verified requirements, asset readiness, integrations, schedule, and support needs.

## Included baseline

1. Discovery and a reviewed brief covering goals, audience, information needs, assets, and constraints.
2. Sitemap and content direction for up to four informational pages.
3. Responsive design and static website implementation within the approved proposal.
4. Basic on-page metadata, heading structure, image alternative text, and technical accessibility review.
5. Build and browser QA, two in-scope revision rounds, handover notes, and a launch-readiness review.

## Excluded unless separately scoped

- Domain, hosting, paid subscriptions, premium fonts, licensed stock assets, and other third-party fees.
- CMS, e-commerce, booking, checkout, online payments, accounts, dashboards, databases, or API integrations.
- WhatsApp or other messaging integrations, analytics, tracking, advertising, campaign management, and automation.
- Logo creation, photography, video, long-form copywriting, translation, and content migration beyond the proposal.
- Ongoing content updates, maintenance, security monitoring, support retainers, and expedited delivery.
- Claims clearance, legal advice, privacy policy drafting, trademark checks, and regulatory compliance certification.

Any requested item in this list requires a written change of scope, schedule, and fee before work begins.

## Commercial operating rules

### Scope and change control

- The signed or otherwise explicitly approved proposal is the source of truth for pages, features, deliverables, responsibilities, schedule, and price.
- A revision changes an approved deliverable within scope. A new page, feature, integration, content direction, or reopened approved milestone is a scope change.
- Work outside scope starts only after written approval of its fee and schedule impact.

### Payment and start conditions

- The project is scheduled after proposal approval and receipt of the 50% deposit.
- The delivery clock starts only after required materials, access, and designated approver details are complete.
- Final payment is due after QA and result approval, before production publication or final account/file handover, as stated in the project proposal.
- Third-party purchases must be approved in advance and should be owned by the client wherever practical.

### Review, acceptance, and warranty

- Each review request should name the decision maker and provide consolidated feedback.
- Acceptance criteria are agreed before implementation and checked during QA.
- The 14-day warranty covers reproducible defects where the delivered implementation does not match the approved scope.
- The warranty excludes new requests, client edits, third-party outages or changes, unsupported environments, compromised credentials, and content supplied by the client.
- Maintenance after the warranty requires a separate agreement; no continuous availability or business-result guarantee is implied.

### Ownership and publication

- The proposal must identify ownership of source code, design files, content, accounts, and reusable studio components.
- The client confirms rights and accuracy for supplied names, claims, logos, photos, and other materials.
- Domain and hosting accounts should remain under the client's control; access must use the minimum permissions needed.
- Publication, indexing, analytics, and contact activation each require explicit authorization. A merged PR is not publication approval.

## Prospect-to-delivery flow

1. **Prospect discovery:** a prospect finds the portfolio or receives an approved introduction; no unsolicited outreach is authorized by this document.
2. **Brief to proposal:** goals, required pages, constraints, budget fit, materials, owners, and acceptance criteria are documented.
3. **Design direction:** visual and content direction is prepared from reviewed information and approved before implementation.
4. **Coding and QA:** implementation, evidence-based checks, and the agreed revision rounds are completed.
5. **Delivery:** approval, payment, ownership, access, documentation, warranty start, and any publication decision are recorded.

AI may assist research, drafting, design direction, implementation, and QA. A human owner remains responsible for verifying facts, permissions, commercial commitments, and publication.

## Contact-channel preparation gate

No email address, WhatsApp number, form, calendar, payment link, or public ordering language is approved yet. Before enabling a channel, the owner must decide and record:

1. The channel and business identity that may be published.
2. Who monitors it, expected response time, business hours, and backup owner.
3. Minimum intake fields and a data-retention/deletion policy.
4. Spam controls, consent wording, privacy notice, and access permissions.
5. A tested disabled/failure state and a rollback method.

Until these decisions are approved, `contact.enabled` remains `false`, website metadata remains `noindex,nofollow`, `robots.txt` continues to disallow crawling, and the service page continues to say that ordering is unavailable.
